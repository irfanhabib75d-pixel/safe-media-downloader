### Full Description (App Store)
Safe Media Downloader is a clean, permission-first media utility built to download high-resolution photos, videos, and audio from platforms that officially authorize third-party media retrieval.

Unlike traditional scrapers, Safe Media Downloader connects exclusively to official platform APIs (such as Wikimedia Commons, Internet Archive, Flickr, and Pexels) to retrieve authorized, public-domain, and Creative Commons licensed media with full creator attribution.

#### ✨ Key Features
- **Permission-First Architecture:** Only connects to official developer APIs that explicitly permit media downloads.
- **Resumable Download Queue:** Start, pause, resume, or cancel active downloads with real-time speed, ETA, and progress metrics.
- **Full Quality Options:** Choose from official resolutions and formats provided directly by the source platform.
- **Creator Attribution & Rights Safeguards:** Displays author names and Creative Commons licenses before saving to your device.
- **Open & Share:** Easily view completed files in your preferred media viewer or share them using the system share sheet.
- **Dual Language Support:** Full support for English and Roman Urdu.
- **Privacy-Focused:** Runs 100% on your device. No user tracking, no third-party ads, and no external tracking servers.

#### 🛡️ Supported Platforms
- **Wikimedia Commons:** Public domain and Creative Commons media via official MediaWiki APIs.
- **Internet Archive:** Historical, educational, and public domain media via the official Archive Metadata API.
- **Flickr:** Creative Commons photos with owner download permissions enabled.
- **Pexels:** High-resolution stock photos and videos under the free Pexels License.
- **Reddit:** Author-hosted public media files (`i.redd.it`).

*Note: Safe Media Downloader does not support YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or other platforms whose Terms of Service prohibit third-party downloading. For unsupported platforms, the app provides a direct link to open the official application.*
